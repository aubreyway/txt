Set theory: westerners tend to think in terms of instrinsic sets, rather than relational sets.

What defines a thing? Is it the qualities and characteristics that the thing has in and of itself, or is it the relationships that a thing has to other things?

Example: What is an apple?
	**Intrinsic:** a red/green fruit that tastes a certain way with a certain texture, is a certain shape, has seeds of a certain size and shape.
	**Relational:** a fruit that comes from an apple tree

*How do you think we normally think of Christians? As members of an intrinsic set or a relational set? How should we think of them?*

**Intrinsic:** a Christian is someone who believes x, y and z
**Relational:** a Christian is someone who is related to Jesus in a particular way

It seems like we often think about evangelism as inviting others to join an intrinsic set (change their beliefs—and maybe also their desires and behaviors—so that they are members of the set). But it seems like we think about discipleship more in relational set terms (changing their behavior—and also their beliefs and desires—so they are more closely following Jesus).
