### Family 3 Ps thoughts

##### Family is a type that points to a kingdom of God reality

Family is a "this age" structure that points towards an "age to come" reality (and we live in both of those ages right now)

As is marriage—points to the relationship between Christ and the church

Jesus says who are my mother and brothers? Those that do the will of God

Jesus says there won't be marriage in the Kingdom

Mark 10.29-30: Jesus said, “Truly, I say to you, there is no one who has left house or brothers or sisters or mother or father or children or lands, for my sake and for the gospel, who will not receive a hundredfold now in this time, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and lands, with persecutions, and in the age to come eternal life.

We have God as our Father, and fellow Christians are our brothers and sisters (Paul also talks about having parents/children in the faith)


##### Family should be defined much more broadly than we are used to

Scripture uses "family" to refer to something much broader than our conception of a nuclear family

Family vs Household: God (and his people)often deals with "households" rather than "families" related by blood. (Gen 7.1; Deut 6.22; Josh 6.25; 2 Sam 6.12; Prov 31; Mark 6.4; Acts 11.4; Gal 6.10; Eph 2.19; 1 Tim 3.15; 1 Pet 4.17)

The church is referred in the epistles more often in terms of household than family

Similarly, brother and sister are used much more broadly than their literal meanings throughout the NT (including during Jesus' ministry—prior to the founding of churches; i.e. sermon on the mount)

Throughout the book of Acts and the epistles it's difficult to find literal uses of familial terms among all the figurative uses

God's family is always growing (Isa 56.3-5; Romans 11.23-24)

Importance of friendship: Prov 18.24 Jn 15.13



The gospel demands that adoption be included in our paradigm of family structures (Rom 8:15)







The family is the fundamental social sphere which God has given us.

The family points forward to an enduring spiritual reality: the community of God's people. Jesus continually refers to God as his (and our) Father (Matt. 6:9), and says that "whoever does the will of God, he is my brother and sister and mother," (Mark 3:35).

Similarly, when speaking of marriage, Paul says, "This mystery is profound, and I am saying that it refers to Christ and the church," (Eph 5:32).

Both, marriage and family, prepare us to understand our relationship to God and his people.
